1 Executive Overview & Problem Statement

In high-velocity engineering teams, burnout and morale degradation are chronic compounding issues rather than overnight events. Traditional agile project management tools (such as Jira, Linear, or Asana) only capture failure retrospectively: when deliverables are missed, sprint commitments fail, or contributors resign.

The Team Burnout & Morale Early Warning Radar is a proactive early warning radar designed to give Project Managers (PMs) actionable, empathetic foresight into team strain 10 to 14 days before missed deadlines or employee turnover occur.

Non-Invasive Architecture Guarantee: The radar introduces zero surveillance software, zero screen tracking, and zero background microphone listening. It operates entirely as an algorithmic aggregator over existing platform metadata: task transition logs, weekend submission timestamps, and daily standup progress notes.

2 The 4 Core Algorithmic Signals

The radar synthesizes four normalized dimensions (0 to 100) extracted from existing platform data:

Signal Dimension Data Origin & Formula Burnout Correlation Rationale
1. Task Churn Index (TCI) Ratio of status oscillation between In Progress, Blocked, and To Do:
TCI = min(100, (State_Flips / Active_Days) * 40)
 Frequent context switching and re-work deplete cognitive stamina without delivering finished value.
2. Overdue & Rollover Load (ORA) Days past end_date + sprint boundary rollover count:
ORA = min(100, (Overdue_Days * 8) + (Rollover_Count * 15))
 Carrying stale tickets from sprint to sprint produces psychological fatigue and feelings of falling behind.
3. Off-Hours & Weekend Activity (OWR) Logs submitted on Sat/Sun or outside 08:30–19:30 local time:
OWR = min(100, (Weekend_Logs / Total_Logs) * 200)
 Direct indicator of workload spillover into personal recovery time, destroying work-life balance.
4. Blocker & Sentiment Friction (BSF) Proportion of has_worked === 0 + negative friction keywords:
BSF = (Blocker_Days_Pct * 0.7) + (Keyword_Friction * 0.3)
 External dependencies (e.g. staging certs, vendor sandboxes, missing credentials) create feelings of helplessness.

3 Composite Scoring Model & Risk Tiers

The composite Burnout Risk Score (BRS) is computed using an empirically weighted linear combination:

BRS = (TCI * 0.25) + (ORA * 0.25) + (OWR * 0.25) + (BSF * 0.25)
Score Risk Tier Observed Team State Recommended PM Action
0 – 30 Optimal Flow Steady progress, low churn, consistent working hours. Maintain current cadence; protect focus blocks.
31 – 60 Moderate Strain Occasional late logs, 1–2 blockers, moderate context switching. Check in during 1-on-1s; verify ticket requirements.
61 – 80 Elevated Fatigue Repeated overdue deliverables, weekend activity spikes. Deprioritize 1–2 tickets; rebalance pod workloads.
81 – 100 Critical Burnout Severe multi-day blocker stall, chronic off-hours logging. Immediate leadership escalation; clear external blocker.

5 Implementation Plan & Rollout Phases

Strict Safety Constraint: Zero modifications to backend databases, SQLite tables, or existing controller APIs. The radar computes its telemetry purely on existing datasets delivered via /projects/all/tasks and /employees/:id/analytics.
Phase Target File / Scope Implementation Deliverables
Phase 1 client/src/utils/burnoutRadarCalculator.js Pure computation engine. Takes raw tasks & logs, outputs normalized dimensions (TCI, ORA, OWR, BSF, BRS) and tailored PM action bullet points.
Phase 2 client/src/components/BurnoutRadarModal.jsx Self-contained modal / card with pure SVG polygon radar chart (no heavy D3/Chart.js dependency), dark/light mode parity, and contributor breakdown.
Phase 3 Employee360View.jsx & OtherWorkspaces.jsx Adds a designated "Burnout & Morale Radar" entry button in PM views to open the radar drawer/modal, preserving all existing KPI cards and tables.
Phase 4 Vite Build & Smoke Test Execute npm run build, verify zero bundle regressions, test backdrop-blur styling and dark mode contrast.

6 Ethical & Psychological Safety Safeguards

  • No Punitive Metrics: The score is never labeled as developer competence or velocity. It is explicitly labeled as "Process & External Friction Risk".
  • Blameless Problem Solving: Alerts emphasize external blockers (e.g. "Blocked by missing staging DB credentials") to direct management focus onto removing impediments.
  • Restricted Visibility: Radar details are restricted to PM and Superuser management views, avoiding cross-contributor comparisons.
